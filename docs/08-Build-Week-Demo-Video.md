# OpenAI Build Week Demo Video

## Objective

Create a clear, credible product demo that helps a reviewer understand SmeAIHub in under two minutes:

> SmeAIHub helps service businesses identify practical AI opportunities, understand their potential impact, and move toward implementation.

The video should show the complete journey from the business problem to an industry-specific diagnosis result. It must accurately distinguish the current deterministic prototype from the planned OpenAI-powered runtime.

## Production Format

- Target length: 1 minute 50 seconds to 2 minutes
- Aspect ratio: 16:9
- Resolution: 1920 × 1080
- Frame rate: 30 fps
- Delivery: MP4, H.264
- Captions: Burned in or included as an accurate caption track
- Narration: Calm, confident, and conversational

## Final Voiceover Script

### 0:00–0:20 — Problem

Service businesses lose valuable time and revenue to repetitive customer questions, fragmented bookings, inconsistent follow-up, and manual operations. But adopting AI still feels too technical for many small and medium-sized teams.

### 0:20–0:34 — Product

SmeAIHub starts with the business, not the technology. It helps restaurants, hotels, spas, and other service businesses identify practical AI opportunities and understand where automation can create the most value.

### 0:34–0:55 — Diagnosis

In this example, Harbor Table is a restaurant with several locations. Its biggest challenge is managing bookings and reservations, and its goal is to automate inquiries, improve guest follow-up, and increase repeat visits.

### 0:55–1:05 — Analysis

SmeAIHub turns that business context into a focused diagnosis workflow, reviewing the profile, matching relevant workflows, and preparing actionable recommendations.

### 1:05–1:34 — Results

The result is an industry-specific report. Harbor Table receives an AI readiness score of eighty-four, prioritized opportunities for reservation automation, review follow-up, and personalized promotions, plus estimated impact of thirty-one hours saved each month and a nineteen percent growth opportunity. From here, the business can book a strategy session or begin another diagnosis.

### 1:34–1:49 — OpenAI

SmeAIHub was designed and built with GPT-5 through Codex as a product and engineering collaborator. This Build Week prototype currently uses deterministic industry recommendations, keeping the demo predictable and business data out of the OpenAI runtime.

### 1:49–2:00 — Vision

Next, we will use the OpenAI Responses API and Structured Outputs to generate live, explainable diagnosis reports. SmeAIHub: every service business deserves an intelligent workforce.

## Safe Recording Workflow

1. Record from the local production-ready interface in a clean browser window.
2. Use only the fictional data listed above.
3. Use the existing honeypot preview path while recording so the interface completes successfully without sending an email or creating a test lead.
4. Restore the honeypot field immediately after recording and never commit a preview value.
5. Decline analytics cookies in the recording browser to avoid generating test analytics events.
6. Confirm that no API keys, email inboxes, developer tools, bookmarks, personal tabs, or notifications appear in frame.

## Recording Checklist

- [ ] Browser zoom is 100% and the window is 16:9.
- [ ] Desktop notifications and menu-bar distractions are disabled.
- [ ] The homepage and Demo are loaded before recording.
- [ ] Fictional form data is ready for consistent typing.
- [ ] The pointer moves slowly and stops during the Thinking state.
- [ ] The result score and each impact metric remain readable long enough.
- [ ] Narration matches the visible screen without dead time.
- [ ] Background music, if used, stays well below the narration.
- [ ] Captions are proofread and use the exact product terminology.
- [ ] The final export is under two minutes and readable at 1080p.

## Editing Notes

- Use `public/submission/build-week-cover.jpg` for the opening and closing frame.
- Prefer clean cuts and short crossfades; avoid decorative transitions.
- Accelerate form entry slightly if needed, but show every selected field clearly.
- Do not accelerate the Thinking state below the point where its steps can be read.
- Hold the Results screen for the longest portion of the video.
- Use `public/submission/ai-diagnosis-results.jpg` only as a fallback cutaway; the primary demo should show the live interface.

## Acceptance Criteria

- The video is no longer than two minutes.
- A reviewer can identify the problem, audience, product flow, and value without additional context.
- The complete Form → Thinking → Results journey is visible.
- Restaurant-specific output is readable and matches the submitted example.
- GPT-5/Codex usage and the current deterministic runtime are described accurately.
- The OpenAI Responses API integration is presented as the next milestone, not as a current capability.
- No personal data, secrets, test email, or analytics event is exposed.
- The video ends with the SmeAIHub name, website, and product vision.
