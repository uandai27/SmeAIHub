# SmeAIHub Design Language

Version: 1.0

Last Updated: July 2026

---

# Purpose

This document defines the visual language and interaction principles of SmeAIHub.

Every page, component, and future product should follow this design language to ensure a consistent user experience.

---

# Brand Personality

SmeAIHub should feel:

- Professional
- Intelligent
- Calm
- Trustworthy
- Modern

We avoid visual noise and unnecessary decoration.

Our interface should communicate confidence through clarity.

---

# Design Principles

## 1. Product before decoration

Every visual element should help users understand the product.

Avoid decorative graphics that do not communicate product value.

---

## 2. Simplicity creates trust

Simple interfaces reduce cognitive load.

Users should immediately understand what the platform does.

---

## 3. Consistency over creativity

Reusable patterns are more valuable than unique pages.

Consistency helps users learn the product faster.

---

## 4. Show the product

Whenever possible, display real product interfaces instead of abstract AI illustrations.

The product is our strongest marketing asset.

---

## 5. White space is part of the design

Generous spacing improves readability and perceived quality.

Do not fill empty space unless it improves understanding.

# Motion

Animations should be subtle.

Fast.

Purposeful.

Never distracting.

---

# Dashboard Philosophy

The product interface is part of the brand.

Dashboards should emphasize:

- clarity
- hierarchy
- business metrics
- customer insights

Avoid unnecessary decoration.

---

# Voice

Our writing should be:

- Clear
- Professional
- Friendly
- Direct

Avoid technical jargon whenever possible.

Speak to business owners, not AI engineers.

---

# Design Goal

Every screen should answer one question:

"How does this help our customers grow their business?"
